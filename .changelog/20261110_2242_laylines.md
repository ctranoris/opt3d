# Laylines to mark 1, the gate and the finish

2026-11-10 22:42

Asked for: a toggle to show laylines at Mark 1, Mark 3 and the Finish
(with a definition of a layline: the line from a target on which a boat
can reach it in one leg, close-hauled upwind, at her gybing angle
downwind).

## What changed

- **`index.html`**: a **Laylines** checkbox in the Race section.
- **`race-course.js`**: six dashed lines on the water, green for
  starboard tack, red for port; `course.setLaylines(on)`,
  `course.layLaylines(windDeg)`. They are laid again whenever the wind
  direction changes.
  - Mark 1: two lines running down and out from the mark at 61 degrees to
    the wind (the fleet's 58 degree close-hauled heading plus about 3
    degrees of leeway), 75 m long.
  - Finish: the port tack line from the left end of the finish line and
    the starboard tack one from the right end, 50 m long; between them a
    boat can cross the line.
  - Gate 3S / 3P: two lines running up from the middle of the gate at 15
    degrees either side of dead downwind (25 for the ILCA), 65 m long.
  - Lengths are 1.6 times these for the ILCA, like the course.
- **`viewer.js`**: `setLaylines()`; ticking the box brings the course out
  if it is not. `OPTi.setLaylines` for scripts.
- **`README.md`**: the control.

## Checked

- In the page (headless Chrome), wind from 000: clicking the checkbox
  turns the course on; the lines start at mark 1, the gate middle and
  the two finish ends, on bearings 119 / 241 from mark 1 and the finish
  and 345 / 015 from the gate, as they should; a screenshot from above
  mark 1 shows the green line to the right and the red to the left; no
  console errors.

## Not done

- The downwind angles (15 and 25 degrees off dead downwind) are a
  judgment, not taken from the boats' polars.
- The lines follow the wind set on the slider, not the passing shifts
  the boats feel, and they do not allow for a boat sailing higher or
  lower than the fleet does.
- No laylines to mark 2 (a reach) or to the start.
- Nothing committed.
