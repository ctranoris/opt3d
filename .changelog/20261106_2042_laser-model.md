# ILCA 4 / Laser 4.7 model

Date: 2026-11-06 20:42

A second boat, built from `init_assets/laser4.7/`. That folder holds the side view, the parts diagram, the exploded-diagram PDF, and the sail and mast photographs.

## New files

- `model/laser-boat.js` builds the boat with `createLaser()` and holds `LASER_SPEC`. The boat has:
  - **Hull and deck:** lofted hull and deck with the flange, a non-slip deck, the cockpit with bailer, the hiking strap and the daggerboard.
  - **Rudder:** a kick-up rudder with an aluminium head, tiller and extension.
  - **Rig:**
    - two-piece mast with the pre-bent 4.7 lower section;
    - gooseneck, vang, outhaul and boom;
    - mid-boom and boom-end blocks, the traveller and the ratchet block;
    - mainsheet run;
    - wind vane.
  - **Sail:** three battens, a window, telltales, and national letters and sail number with port and starboard at different heights. The grey "4.7" is on the sail, but no class logo or wordmark.
  - **For the viewer:** the boat carries its own sailor pose, part labels and sail area.
- `model/boats.js` is the registry of boat types. It picks this page load's type from `?boat=` in the address, then from the `opt3d_boat` cookie, and defaults to the Optimist.

## Wired in

- **viewer:**
  - the water shader's hull cut-out and wake use the boat's own length;
  - the float, speed, labels and readout follow the boat type;
  - so do the GLB file name, boom drag and camera distance;
  - the page title and heading show the boat's name;
  - a new **Boat** selector in the panel saves the choice in the cookie and reloads the page.
- **fleet:** the fleet is built from the same boat type, with that boat's speed, float and sail marks.
- **sailor:** the sailor takes a size, a pose from the boat and the tiller extension length from the boat.
- **opti-rig:** `updateRig`, `updateMainsheet` and `setSailMarks` hand off to the rig's own methods when it has them.
- **race-course:** the course scales with the boat (×1.6 for the Laser), so the start slots fit the longer hulls.
- **scene-extras:** the hull table now has 87 stations and takes a station function, so it can hold either hull.
