# Sound from the sounds folder

Date: 2026-11-03 17:35

## What changed

1. **New `sound.js`.** It plays the recordings in `sounds/` through the Web
   Audio API.

   Loops run all the time, faded by the state of the boat each frame:

   | Sound | File | Follows |
   |---|---|---|
   | Water | `sailing_sound.mp3` | Boat speed: louder, and a little faster, as she speeds up. |
   | Luffing sail | `freesound_community-sailboat-sail-flapping-…mp3` | The sail not drawing: in irons, mid-tack, or sheeted out too far. |
   | Wind in the rig | `flag_sound.mp3` | Apparent wind. |

   One-shot sounds:

   | Sound | File | When |
   |---|---|---|
   | Mainsheet | `mainsheet_sound_1.mp3` / `_2.mp3`, picked at random, with a slightly varied pitch | The boom moves faster than 18° a second (trimming, easing, swinging across). There is a short cooldown between plays. |
   | Sail filling | `sail_deploy.mp3` | The sail fills on the other side after a tack or gybe. It plays at most once every 3 s. |

   - Browsers only allow sound after the user touches the page, so the
     audio starts on the first click or key press.
   - It all gets quieter as the camera pulls back from the boat, down to
     20%.
   - It pauses while the tab is hidden.
2. **"Sound" checkbox** in the panel, on by default. `OPTi.setSound` and
   `OPTi.sound` are exposed for tests, including `levels()` and
   `played`.
3. **README:** a Sound row has been added to the features table.

## Verified

In headless Chrome, with wind from the north:

| Situation | Speed | Water | Luffing | One-shots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beam reach | 3.1 kn | 0.72 | 0 | — |
| In irons | 0.3 kn | 0.16 | 0.48 | mainsheet, as the boom came in |
| Tacked to 60° | 2.3 kn | 0.56 | 0 | mainsheet, then sail filling |

- Unticking Sound mutes everything.
- No page errors, and no warnings about loading the files.
