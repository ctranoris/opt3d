# Mountains cut down to a few islands

Date: 2026-11-03 17:15

## What changed

1. **Most of the mountains are gone.** The three ridge lines that ran
   round much of the horizon have been replaced by four separate islands,
   each on its own bearing, with open sea horizon everywhere else:

   | Bearing (three.js angle) | Distance | Width | Peak |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 205° | 820 m | 14° | 55 m |
   | 228° | 640 m | 5° | 26 m |
   | 318° | 760 m | 9° | 48 m |
   | 75° | 700 m | 7° | 34 m |

   - Each island is a smooth hump leaning to one side, with gentle ridged
     noise on its outline.
   - The rock is lighter on the slopes that face the sun and is mixed
     toward the horizon haze, more for the farther islands. The foot fades
     into the sea haze, as before.
   - There is no snow any more.
2. **The checkbox is renamed "Islands".** Its id is still `mountainsChk`,
   and `OPTi.setMountains` still works.
3. **README:** the Mountains row is now an Islands row.

## Verified

- Renders from a low camera looking north, east, south and west.
  - The islands sit on the horizon with gaps of open sea between them.
  - The outlines are no longer spiky.
- No page errors.
