# Lower islands

Date: 2026-11-03 17:45

## What changed

The islands were too tall, so their peak heights in `mountains.js` are
roughly halved:

| Island | Bearing | Peak before | Peak now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large, far | 205° | 55 m | 26 m |
| Small | 228° | 26 m | 16 m |
| North-west | 318° | 48 m | 22 m |
| South-east | 75° | 34 m | 16 m |

- The base of each island's hump was lowered from 6 m to 3 m.
- The islands now rise only about 1° above the horizon, so they read as
  low land far off rather than mountains.
- README: the Islands row has been updated.

## Verified

Renders from a low camera looking west and south show the islands as low
hazy humps on the horizon. There are no page errors.
