# Smooth boom under Auto sail

Request: the boom moved in small steps instead of smoothly when sailing
around.

## Cause

`bestBoom` in `sailing.js` scans boom angles in 1.5° steps, so Auto sail's
target moved in 1.5° jumps, and the viewer chased it at a constant 70°/s:
a visible tick each time. On a broad reach (apparent wind about 123°) the
target also jumped by 11° between two nearly equal angles.

## Fix

- `sailing.js`: `bestBoom` fits a parabola through the best scanned angle
  and its neighbours, so the result varies continuously. The off-wind floor
  is now `(awa - 100) * 4` up to 90°, which removes the 11° jump.
- `viewer.js`: under Auto sail the boom eases into its target (rate
  proportional to the distance, up to 70°/s) instead of moving at a fixed
  rate.

## Checked

In Node, sweeping the apparent wind angle in 0.1° steps: the largest change
in the boom target fell from 11.3° to 0.8°. The polar is unchanged to within
0.04 kn. Not re-run in the browser.
