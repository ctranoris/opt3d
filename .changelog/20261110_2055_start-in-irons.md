# Start: no drifting astern, helm works in irons

2026-11-10 20:55

Report: at the start of a race our Optimist is slow and seems to go a
little backwards.

## Cause

- Lined up head to wind for the 10 s countdown, the fleet's boats were
  held still but ours was left to the physics: the wind on hull and
  flogging sail carried her astern, 0.64 kn and 2 m back by the gun in
  8 kn (1.7 kn and 6 m in 16 kn).
- Going astern the rudder worked backwards and cancelled the sculling, so
  the helm key turned her the wrong way, weakly, then back: in a
  simulation with the helm held over for 20 s after the gun she never got
  out of irons and was 8 m astern.

## What changed

- **`sailing.js`** (`stepBoat`): `helm` is now the way she is to turn.
  Going astern (`hull.astern`, with a little hysteresis around 0.1 m/s)
  the sailor puts the tiller the other way and sculls that way, so flow
  and sculling add up instead of cancelling. Applies to the fleet too.
- **`viewer.js`**: after Re-lay course our boat is held stopped, head to
  wind, through the countdown, until the gun or the first touch of a helm
  or sheet key. After the gun, while she is still within 35 degrees of the
  wind, the race hint says to bear away (A or D).
- **`README.md`**: the countdown hold, the helm keys astern.

## Checked

- Simulation in Node (Optimist, 8 kn, Auto sail, helm over at the gun):
  held through the countdown she is at 48 degrees off the wind 4 s after
  the gun, 1 kn at 6 s, 2.1 kn at 10 s, having lost 0.5 m. Not held (2 m
  astern at the gun) she now gets out just the same; at 16 kn, from 1.7 kn
  astern, she is sailing at 2 kn 4 s after the gun.
- `node .tools/fleet-race.js optimist 8`: all twelve finish (364-439 s),
  no console errors. `node .tools/sail.js`: no console errors.
