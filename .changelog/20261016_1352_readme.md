# README, with every claim checked against the build

`README.md` covers what the project is, where the model lives, and how to take
it into other applications — the three things the request asked for. Written
against the actual files rather than from memory, which turned up a number of
statements I would otherwise have published and were wrong.

## Corrected while writing

- **The three.js import style.** I had written that the model modules use the
  bare specifier `'three'`. They do not — `opti-hull.js`, `opti-rig.js` and
  `opti-boat.js` each import `'../lib/three.module.js'` by relative path, and
  only `viewer.js` uses the bare specifier plus the importmap. The README now
  says so, and warns that copying the modules into a project that also
  resolves `'three'` loads two copies of the library.
- **`updateMainsheet()` takes the cleat in rig-local space**, not the boat
  space `createBoat()` reports. The sample had to grow the same
  `matrixWorld`/`invert()` conversion `viewer.js` does. Ran the finished
  snippet in the real page to confirm: `sailTris 1560`, `sheetPts 26`, boom
  22.2° at `boomOut: 0.3` — exactly 0.3 of the 74° of travel, so the parameter
  means what the comment says.
- **Peak height is 2.175 m above the baseline**, not the 2.34 m I had noted
  down earlier. Recomputed from `rigCorners()` through the rig's rake and
  position; foot 1.930 m and head→peak 1.220 m both still match `SPEC`.
- **The camera paragraph was wrong twice over.** I had written that the
  default sits windward and that the leeward preset "reads worst" because the
  camera looks down the sail's chord and sees a thin blade. `CAMS.quarter` is
  at `z = -3.5` and `leeward` at `z = +4.6`, and the boom eases to starboard —
  so quarter and windward are the *same* side, and both broadside presets show
  the sail full-face. Looked at `shot_cam_leeward.png` and
  `shot_cam_windward.png` to confirm before rewriting; the real distinction
  between them is which face of the cloth you see, which matters because the
  mirrored-back-face export caveat is only visible from one of them.
- **Material count.** Listed 18 names under "All 15 materials". `wood`, `gold`
  and `rope` are defined in `makeMaterials()` but used nowhere, so they never
  reach the export — the list is now the 15 that actually appear.
- **Vertex colours** are on the hull *and* the five deck meshes, not the hull
  alone.
- **`--sheet` on the CLI is the eased fraction**, the inverse of the slider's
  percentage — `--sheet 0.85` is not the viewer's "85%". Worth stating
  explicitly next to the example that uses it.
